Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026.

Magica Uploader is operated solely by the owner of the Magica Popshop YouTube channel. The application accesses the owner's own YouTube account through the YouTube Data API only to upload, manage, and delete the owner's own videos on the owner's own channel.

The application does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data of any third party. It does not use analytics or advertising trackers. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's private server and are used only for the channel operations described above.

The owner may revoke the application's access at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings, which immediately disables the stored tokens.
